# A Standardized Framework for the Number of Flaps n² 22.5°-induced Bird Base Compounds Produce when Tiled within a Square
### Abstract
#### 
A system of proofs that lead to a standardized formula to evaluate the number of flaps a compound of ‘n²’ bird bases yield on a square. For brevity, the proofs in this paper assume a working knowledge of basic geometric properties, coupled with trigonometric principles. This paper solely analyses the effect of tiling petal-like bird base flaps with a visible area of 10.354% of the total square area. Empirically testing crease patterns on an Origami Simulator for their flat-folded patterns, it concludes with a precise formula for any n² bird base tiles.

### Keywords
#### 
Origami, Trigonometry, Square tiling, Tree theory, Structural design
